02 • Flagship SaaS & GDSS Ecosystem

Production Platforms Operated Under MTN-SPEC

1. The National Mineral Intelligence Hub — Mineral Mapping GDSS

nationalmineralintelligencehub.co.zw • mtnspec.co.zw/mineral-hub

A sovereign Geospatial Decision Support System (GDSS) replacing hazardous "blind digging" with 10m–20m Copernicus Sentinel-2 spectral alteration pipelines (Iron Oxide, Hydrothermal SWIR Clay, Ferrous Silicates, Gossan & NDVI anomaly mapping), live bullion/PGM spot feeds, paid youth STEM GPS ground-truthing with EcoCash QR escrow, and integrated Akello Mining Safety & ESG compliance training.

2. AgriShield — Advanced Remote Sensing & Deep-Learning Crop GDSS

agrishield.co.zw

Fuses 12+ multi-sensor Earth observation indices (FLDAS Root-Zone Soil Moisture, CHIRPS SPI Rainfall, MODIS & Sentinel-2 NDVI, NDRE, VCI, and VHI) with Deep Learning and Random Forest ensembles trained on 25+ years of climate records—delivering early crop-stress alerts and tamper-proof parametric crop insurance triggers.

3. Change It — Urban Spatial Intelligence & Cashless Digital Change

changeit.co.zw • mtnspec.co.zw/changeit.html

Applies urban spatial intelligence across high-density transit corridors and termini while powering sub-second HMAC-SHA256 QR fare checkout and multi-network digital change wallets—permanently eliminating Zimbabwe's everyday small-denomination cash change crisis for commuters, conductors, and merchants.
