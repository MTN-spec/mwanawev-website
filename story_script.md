# Paywega: The End of the "Change" Crisis

**Format:** This script is designed for AI Video Generation tools. Each scene contains a `Visual Prompt` to generate the video clip and `Audio/Script` for the voiceover.

---

## Scene 1: The Morning Rush
**Visual Prompt:**
> A cinematic, hyper-realistic shot of a busy bus rank in Harare, Zimbabwe, early morning. Golden hour lighting. Dozens of white commuter omnibuses (combis) lined up. Crowds of people walking fast, eager to get to work. Dust rising slightly in the golden light. Atmosphere of energy and urgency.

**Audio (Narrator - Deep, warm, local accent):**
"Harare never sleeps. The rhythm of the city starts with the morning rush. We are a people on the move, chasing dreams, building our future."

---

## Scene 2: The Stoppage
**Visual Prompt:**
> Close up inside a combi. A passenger's hand holding a crisp $5 USD note. The conductor (gwindi), wearing a blue work suit, looks stressed and shakes his head. He opens his hands to show they are empty. Other passengers look annoyed and look at their watches. High tension.

**Audio (Narrator):**
"But every day, that rhythm hits a wall. The dreaded words: *'Handina change'* (I don't have change)."

---

## Scene 3: The Frustration
**Visual Prompt:**
> Split screen or montage.
> 1. A conductor shouting at a passenger who is refusing to get off without change.
> 2. Passengers pairing up, trying to split a large note, looking confused and frustrated.
> 3. A person walking away from a combi looking disappointed because they didn't have small notes.

**Audio (Narrator):**
"It stalls our journey. It wastes our time. We fight for receipts, we pair up with strangers, or worse—we lose our money. The change crisis is not just an inconvenience; it’s a barrier."
